Practice prompts

  1. Lip trills, 1 minute. A buzzy brrr sliding up from your talking pitch to a light high note and floating back down.
  2. Sirens up and down. Play the siren guide and follow it on an ng or oo sound, watching the line climb through the pink band.
  3. Mm-hmm at your target. Play the center tone, then hum a friendly mm-hmm right on it, 5 times. Keep it light, not pushed.
  4. Good morning, Mistress. Say it in that lifted, bright place, smiling slightly. Aim for the needle to stay in your band.
  5. Read a sentence. "The pretty pink sweater is soft and warm on chilly mornings." Keep 60% or more of it in your band.

Gentle rules

  • Never strain or push: light and easy beats high and tight.
  • Sip water before and during practice.
  • Stop right away if your throat hurts, tickles or goes hoarse, and rest.
  • Small daily sessions of 5 to 10 minutes work better than one long one.
  • Watch brightness and pitch together; a bright, forward sound at a moderate pitch reads more feminine than a high, dark one.
